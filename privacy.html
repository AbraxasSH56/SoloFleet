Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

This page is the landing page for SoloFleet, a personal showcase project published as open source under the MIT licence. There is no account, no sign-up and no form on this site.

Nothing loads until you accept

When you first visit, a bar at the bottom asks whether you accept analytics and advertising tags. Until you click Accept, none of the tags below are loaded. If you click Reject, the page works exactly the same. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name sf_consent so we don't ask again. You can change it any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the home page.

What the tags are and why

The only purpose is to see which posts and ads bring people here and how many of them go on to the GitHub repository. If you accept, these load through Google Tag Manager:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google): pages viewed, time on page, clicks on the GitHub buttons, approximate location and device type. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4. Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Ads conversion tag (Google): records that a visitor who came from a Google ad clicked through to GitHub. Ad personalisation is switched off. How Google uses data from ads.
  • Reddit Pixel (Reddit): records a page visit and a click through to GitHub, so we can see whether a Reddit ad worked. Reddit's privacy policy.

These companies set cookies or similar identifiers and process the data under their own policies, linked above. We do not collect your name, email address or any other contact detail, and we do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Links to campaign details

Links in our posts and ads carry tags such as utm_source=reddit in the address. They say which post or ad you came from. They do not identify you.

How to say no or change your mind

  • Click Reject on the bar, or “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the home page and then Reject. If you had accepted before, the page reloads without the tags.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to be asked again.
  • Google offers an opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics.

How long data is kept

Google Analytics data is kept for the shortest period Google Analytics 4 offers (2 months). Ad platform data is kept under Google's and Reddit's own policies.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions

Open an issue on the SoloFleet repository. Please don't post personal details there; issues are public.
